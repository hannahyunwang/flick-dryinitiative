Stage 1: Literature Review

Our background review brings together hand-drying research, engineering descriptions, environmental assessments, and the popular ‘flick and fold’ demonstration. It guides our comparisons of dryer running time and the resulting energy savings.

MECHANISM ≠ EFFECT SIZE

How much water comes off?

Flicking removes excess surface water before drying. Our comparisons measure how much this reduces dryer running time.

FAIR COMPARISON

Does the dryer run for less time?

People judge dryness by feel. Participants in both conditions stop when their hands feel dry, reflecting everyday use. We compare dryer running times using matched trials and comparable starting conditions.

REAL-WORLD VALUE

Does it save useful resources?

We compare dryer running time and calculate the corresponding energy savings, while recording total task time separately. The amount saved can vary with the dryer, person, starting wetness, and flicking technique.

What the review contributes

The review identifies relevant prior work and connects water removal, dryer running time, and resource savings.

Our evidence pathway

  1. Background review: identify the mechanism, prior evidence, and unanswered questions.
  2. Stage 2 archive analysis: Report the observed within-set runtime differences, sample sizes, and measurement accuracy.
