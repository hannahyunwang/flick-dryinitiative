Share the habit with your community

Is your business, school, or community centre interested? Display a Flick-dry sticker in your washrooms (print your own, or ask us for one at contact@flick-dryinitiative.org), or share our hand-dryer and paper-towel infographics (PNG) through your newsletter, social media, events, or other channels.
